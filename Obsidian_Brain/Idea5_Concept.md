# Idea 5: Glow-in-the-Dark Markers + UV Flash

## The Core Concept
A hybrid of Idea 1 (CV markers) and Idea 2 (flash), designed specifically for dark arenas.

Players wear markers made with **fluorescent / glow-in-the-dark paint**. The gun has a **UV (blacklight) LED** that "charges" and illuminates these markers, making them glow brightly even in a pitch-black room. The phone camera can then read the glowing marker.

## How It Works
1. **Target Markers:** Print or paint each player's unique pattern (ArUco marker or large number) using fluorescent/glow paint on their vest. In normal darkness, these are dim or invisible.
2. **The Gun:** Contains a focused UV LED (blacklight). When the trigger is pulled:
   - The UV LED fires a focused beam.
   - If it hits a fluorescent marker, the marker **glows brightly** for a moment.
   - Simultaneously, the Bluetooth trigger tells the shooter's phone to fire.
3. **The Phone Camera:** Detects the sudden bright glow of the marker in the otherwise dark frame. Since the marker has a unique pattern/color, the phone identifies which player was hit.
4. **Obstruction:** UV light can't pass through walls. If you can't illuminate the marker, you can't hit them.

## Why Consider This?
- **Works in the dark** — fluorescent markers glow when hit with UV, creating their own light.
- **Solves "who shot whom"** — the TARGET's phone reads its OWN marker being illuminated (or the shooter's phone sees the glow).
- **Looks incredibly cool** — glowing patterns in a dark arena are visually stunning.
