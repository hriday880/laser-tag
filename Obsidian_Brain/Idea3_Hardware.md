# Idea 3: IR LED + Phone Camera — Hardware

## The IR Gun
**Materials per gun:**
- Cheap plastic toy gun: ~100 INR
- 3x IR LEDs (940nm, from old TV remotes or bulk order): ~10 INR
- Bluetooth Selfie Remote: ~60 INR
- Small 555 timer IC or ATtiny85 (to generate unique pulse codes): ~30 INR
- Resistors, wires, small breadboard: ~15 INR
- 2x AA batteries + holder: ~20 INR
- Cardboard tube / PVC pipe (to focus the IR beam): ~10 INR

**Assembly:**
1. Wire 3 IR LEDs in parallel with a current-limiting resistor.
2. Mount them inside a short cardboard tube (acts as a crude lens/focus barrel) at the gun's muzzle.
3. Wire the IR LEDs through a 555 timer circuit configured to pulse a unique pattern when the trigger microswitch is closed.
4. Simultaneously, wire the trigger to the Bluetooth remote to notify the shooter's phone.

## The Vest
Same as other ideas: elastic strap chest harness with clear phone pouch. Camera facing out.
