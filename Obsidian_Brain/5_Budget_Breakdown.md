## Cost Saving Tips:
1. **Scavenge:** Ask friends for old wired earphones with a mic button. If a phone has a headphone jack, you can wire the earphone button directly to the gun trigger instead of using a Bluetooth remote. This eliminates batteries, pairing issues, and saves 60 INR per gun! (Though many modern phones lack audio jacks).
2. **Cardboard Vests:** Instead of elastic harnesses, you can make the chest plates out of painted Amazon cardboard boxes strapped with cheap twine, dropping vest costs to near 0.
