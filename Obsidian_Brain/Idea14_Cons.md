# Idea 14: The AR Sniper (Periscope) — Cons

## Problems

### 🟡 Mirror Alignment & Quality
- The 45-degree mirror needs to be aligned reasonably well, otherwise the camera looks at the sky or the floor.
- Cheap plastic mirrors can distort the image or reduce the light entering the camera. In a dark arena, losing light is bad.
- **Mitigation:** Use real glass craft mirrors (very cheap in craft stores) and build the periscope hood carefully so it locks into a 45° angle perfectly.

### 🟡 Build Complexity (Crafting 12 Guns)
- You have to gut 12 toy guns, wire 12 earphone buttons behind the mechanical triggers, and glue 12 periscope mounts.
- It will take a weekend of DIY arts, crafts, and hot glue.
- **Mitigation:** It's a fun DIY project! Assembly line it with a friend.

### 🟡 Audio Jack Extinction
- Many modern phones no longer have 3.5mm headphone jacks.
- **Mitigation:** You may need to buy a bulk pack of cheap USB-C to 3.5mm and Lightning to 3.5mm dongles, adding ~₹50-100 per gun. Alternatively, revert to the Bluetooth remote (₹60) taped inside the gun behind the trigger, which works universally wirelessly.

### 🟡 Added Weight
- A phone + gun + mount is heavier than just a phone. Players' arms might get tired faster.
- **Mitigation:** Use lightweight plastic toy guns.

### 🟢 Minor Issues
- **Screen Glare:** Looking at a screen lying flat on top of the gun while overhead lights are on can cause glare. **Mitigation:** The arena is dark, so this won't be an issue.

## What This Idea Gets Right
- ✅ Incredible immersion (feels like a real gun).
- ✅ Safest orientation for the phone (flat, less prone to breaking).
- ✅ Camera naturally aligns with the barrel.
- ✅ Retains all the 10/10 tracking and identification benefits of Idea 9.
- ✅ Fits the budget.

## Verdict
**This is the definitive version of the project.** It combines the flawless software tracking of Idea 9 with the physical satisfaction and safety of a real laser tag gun. The cons are entirely DIY/crafting challenges, not technical impossibilities.
