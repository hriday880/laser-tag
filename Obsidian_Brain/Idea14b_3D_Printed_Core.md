# 💡 Idea 14b: The 3D Printed Core (The Ultimate Optimization)

> **Taking the Periscope Gun (Idea 14) and replacing the toy gun chassis with a custom 3D-printed modular core.**

## The Trap: Printing the Whole Gun
Your first instinct might be to 3D print 12 full-size assault rifles. **Do not do this.** 
- A full-size rifle takes ~300g–500g of filament and 15+ hours to print. 
- For 12 guns, that's ~4.8kg of PLA (₹3,500–₹4,000 just in plastic) and nearly **8 days of non-stop printing**. It blows your budget and your timeline.

## The Solution: The "Core Module" + PVC
Instead of printing the whole gun, you design and 3D print **only the complex part** (The Core Module) and use cheap PVC pipes for the barrel and stock!

### 1. The 3D Printed Core (Takes ~2 hours, 100g of PLA)
You design a single, compact block that houses:
- The universal phone clamp slot.
- A perfectly angled 45-degree slot to slide the craft mirror into (no more guessing or hot glue messes!).
- A pistol grip.
- Socket holes on the front and back to securely jam in standard 1-inch PVC pipes.

### 2. The Capacitive Trigger (The Real Genius of 3D Printing)
Since you are designing the trigger mechanism, you can eliminate the Bluetooth remote (₹60) AND the earphone wire (₹40)! 
- You print a mechanical trigger on a simple pen spring. 
- When pulled, a lever swings up and physically **taps the phone screen**. 
- To make the screen register the tap, you line the trigger and the lever with **copper tape or aluminum foil**. The electrical capacitance of the player's finger travels through the foil and taps the on-screen "FIRE" button.
- **Cost of trigger: ₹5** (spring + foil). Latency: Zero.

### 3. The PVC Body
- Cut a 1-foot section of PVC pipe, spray paint it matte black, and jam it into the front of your 3D printed core. Instant sniper barrel.
- Cut another section for a shoulder stock and stick it in the back.
- Total cost for PVC per gun: ~₹40.

## Why This Wins Everything
- **Cheaper than buying toy guns:** Drops the total project cost to ₹2,676.
- **Flawless Mirror Alignment:** The 3D model guarantees the 45-degree mirror is perfectly aligned with the camera every single time.
- **Zero Electronic Triggers:** The capacitive screen-tap trigger is a masterpiece of cheap engineering. No pairing, no wires, no batteries.
- **Modular:** If a player breaks a PVC barrel, you just slide a new ₹20 pipe in. The 3D printed core stays safe.
