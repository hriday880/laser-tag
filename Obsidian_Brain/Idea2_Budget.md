*Note: This puts us slightly over budget (by 200 INR). To bring it back under 4,000 INR:*

## Cost-Cutting Measures
1. **Ditch the Bluetooth:** Instead of a Bluetooth remote, wire an old **headphone jack mic button** directly to the gun's trigger. The wire runs from the gun to the phone on the player's chest. This saves 720 INR on remotes and batteries, bringing the total cost down to **3,480 INR**!
2. **Bulk Flashlights:** Buy cheap keychain LED lights in bulk from a wholesale market instead of tactical flashlights.
