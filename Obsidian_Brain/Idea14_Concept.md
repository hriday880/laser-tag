# 💡 Idea 14: The AR Sniper (Universal Periscope Mount)

> **Takes the flawless tracking of Idea 9 and wraps it in a true, physical Laser Tag rifle.**

## The Key Insight
Instead of awkwardly holding a phone like you're filming a TikTok, we mount the phone **FLAT on top of a physical toy gun**. 
To allow the camera to "see" forward while lying flat, we use a **simple 45-degree mirror (a periscope)**. The screen faces up, acting as your holographic sight / ammo counter.

## How It Works
1. **The Gun Chassis:** Buy a cheap, large plastic toy rifle (₹150). 
2. **The Universal Mount:** Attach a cheap, spring-loaded universal phone clamp (like a basic car mount or tripod grip, ₹50) flat on the top rail of the gun. This holds ANY phone securely.
3. **The Periscope Hood:** A small piece of PVC pipe or thick cardboard glued in front of the phone clamp. Inside is a small craft mirror (₹10) angled exactly at 45 degrees.
   - The phone camera looks *down* into the mirror, which reflects the *forward* view.
   - The software simply flips the camera feed vertically so it looks normal on screen.
   - Because the mirror is slightly wider than a phone lens, it works for **any phone** (whether the camera is in the center, left, or right!).
4. **The Physical Trigger (The Magic):**
   - Take a scavenged wired earphone (with a mic/play button).
   - Gut the toy gun. Position the earphone's mic button directly behind the toy gun's mechanical plastic trigger.
   - Run the 3.5mm jack (or a cheap Type-C/Lightning adapter) out of the gun to plug into the phone.
   - When you pull the physical rifle trigger → it clicks the mic button → the phone registers a "Volume/Media" press → BOOM, laser fired.
5. **The Vest:** Players wear the same cheap LED pulsing beacons on their chest/back from Idea 9.

## Why This is the Ultimate Experience
- **100% Proper Laser Tag Feel:** You are holding a rifle, looking at a screen scope, pulling a mechanical trigger.
- **Phone is Safe:** Lying flat on the barrel (or even enclosed in a slot) means no dropping, no screen-smashing if you bump into a wall.
- **Universal Fit:** The spring mount + wide mirror periscope adapts to an iPhone 15 Pro, a budget Android, or anything in between.
- **Zero Latency:** A wired audio-jack trigger is faster and more reliable than Bluetooth. No pairing headaches!
