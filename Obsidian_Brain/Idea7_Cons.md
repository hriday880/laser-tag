# Idea 7: Laser Pointer + Phone Screen — Cons

## Critical Problems

### 🔴 Tiny Target Area
- A phone screen is only ~6 inches tall. At 10 meters distance, hitting a 6-inch target with a handheld laser pointer while both players are moving is nearly impossible.
- Traditional laser tag vests have sensors covering the entire torso. A phone screen is a fraction of that.
- **Gameplay will be extremely frustrating** — shots will almost never land.

### 🔴 Eye Safety — SERIOUS RISK
- Laser pointers aimed at the chest level WILL inevitably hit players in the eyes.
- In a dark room with dilated pupils, even a Class 2 laser can cause retinal damage.
- **This is a genuine safety hazard and potential liability.** Especially dangerous with 12 excited players running around in the dark.

### 🔴 No Player Identification
- A red laser dot is just a red dot. There's no way for the target's phone to know WHICH gun fired the laser.
- All laser pointers look the same to a camera.
- **Workaround:** Pair with Bluetooth timestamp matching (Idea 2 hybrid), but this brings back all of Idea 2's problems.

### 🟡 Front Camera Quality
- Front cameras on budget phones are typically lower quality (2-5MP, narrow FOV) compared to rear cameras.
- Detecting a tiny laser dot on the screen via the front camera is technically challenging.

### 🟡 Screen Damage
- Powerful laser pointers can potentially damage phone screens or camera sensors with prolonged direct exposure.

### 🟢 Minor Issues
- **Laser legality:** In some areas, laser pointers above certain power levels have restrictions.
- **Smoke/haze required:** Laser beams are only visible in dusty/smoky air, adding setup complexity.

## Verdict
**Eye safety alone kills this idea.** The tiny target area makes gameplay nearly impossible, and there's no way to identify the shooter. As cool as visible lasers look, this is too dangerous and impractical.
