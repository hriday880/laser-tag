# Hardware Design (Light Strike)

We still need to build 12 sets for under 4,000 INR (Max ~333 INR per set). 

## 1. The Light Gun
**Materials:**
- Cheap Plastic Toy Gun: ~100 INR
- Zoomable LED Flashlight (or cheap laser pointer with a diffusing lens): ~80 INR
- Bluetooth Selfie Remote: ~60 INR
- Microswitch & Wire: ~20 INR
- Colored Cellophane/Gel (e.g., Red or Green): ~10 INR

**The Build:**
1. Mount the flashlight inside or on top of the toy gun barrel. Focus the beam so it makes a ~2-foot wide circle at a distance of 15 feet. Cover the lens with the colored cellophane (so it shoots a specific color).
2. Wire a single microswitch behind the gun's physical trigger.
3. Connect this microswitch to **both** the flashlight circuit AND the Bluetooth remote's circuit. 
4. **Result:** One physical trigger pull closes both circuits simultaneously. The flashlight flashes, and the Bluetooth remote clicks.

## 2. The Vest
**Materials:**
- Elastic Straps + Plastic Buckles: ~50 INR
- Clear Waterproof Mobile Pouch: ~30 INR

**The Build:**
Exactly the same as previous ideas. Glue/sew the straps to the pouch to create a secure chest harness. The phone sits inside, camera exposed, screen facing the player's chest (or out, if you want it to flash colors when they die).
