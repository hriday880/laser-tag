# 💡 Idea 12: The Heist — Phone-as-Gun + NFC Tag Assassinations

> **A completely different game genre. Not shooting — silent assassinations.**

## The Core Concept
Throw away the idea of "shooting" at range. Instead, build a **stealth assassination / tag game** in the dark.

## How It Works
1. **Each player has their phone in hand** (flashlight OFF, screen dimmed).
2. **Each player wears a cheap NFC sticker** on their back (between shoulder blades — the "kill zone").
3. **To "kill" someone:** Sneak up behind them and tap your phone against their NFC sticker. The phone reads the NFC tag (which contains the victim's Player ID) and registers the kill with the server.
4. **The twist:** You must be BEHIND them. NFC requires ~4cm contact. This forces true stealth gameplay — creeping through a dark arena, listening for footsteps, ambushing from behind.
5. **Defense:** The phone's front-facing camera + proximity sensor detects if someone is approaching from the front. If so, it warns you (subtle vibration). You can't be "killed" from the front — you can block or run.

## Why This Is Radically Different (and Maybe Better)
- **Zero projectiles, zero sensors** — just NFC stickers (₹5 each).
- **Works in TOTAL darkness** — no cameras needed for detection. NFC is touch-based.
- **Intense gameplay** — stealth, paranoia, listening, heart-pounding sneaking.
- **Cheapest possible build** — NFC stickers are ₹5 each. Total project cost under ₹500.
- **No line-of-sight issues** — you must physically touch someone. Walls naturally block you.
- **No phone compatibility issues** — virtually all modern smartphones have NFC.
