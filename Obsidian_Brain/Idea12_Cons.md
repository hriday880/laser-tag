# Idea 12: The Heist (NFC Assassinations) — Cons

## Problems

### 🔴 Not Laser Tag
- This is fundamentally a **different game**. It's stealth tag, not a shooter.
- Players expecting a laser-tag-like shooting experience will be disappointed.
- No ranged combat, no "gun feel", no pew-pew satisfaction.

### 🟡 Physical Contact Required
- Players must physically touch each other's backs. This could lead to:
  - Rough physical play (shoving, grabbing).
  - Discomfort for some players (personal space issues).
  - Injury risk in a dark room (collisions, tripping).

### 🟡 NFC Read Reliability
- NFC reads require the phone to be within ~4cm AND properly aligned with the sticker.
- In a fast, panicky situation (target is running away), getting a clean NFC read is unreliable.
- Some phone cases block NFC. Players may need to remove cases.

### 🟡 Limited Game Depth
- Without ranged combat, game modes are limited. No team deathmatch, no capture the flag, no king of the hill.
- Essentially just "last person standing" or "most kills wins."

### 🟢 Minor Issues
- **NFC availability:** Most modern phones have NFC, but some budget Android phones don't.
- **Game pacing:** Could be very slow if players are overly cautious (everyone just hiding).

## What This Idea Gets Right
- ✅ Absurdly cheap (₹180 total)
- ✅ Perfect in total darkness
- ✅ Zero electronics complexity
- ✅ Genuinely terrifying gameplay (dark room, silent assassins)
- ✅ Works on almost all phones

## Verdict
**Amazing as a SIDE MODE or party game variant.** Not a replacement for proper laser tag, but an incredible complement. Run it as "Round 2" after a laser tag session, or as a standalone party game. The ₹180 cost means you can build this ALONGSIDE another idea.
