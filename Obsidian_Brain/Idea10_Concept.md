# 💡 Idea 10: Ghost Mode — Phone-as-Gun + Retroreflective Tape

> **Zero electronics on the vest. The target is completely passive.**

## The Core Concept
Same "phone-as-gun" paradigm as [[Idea9_Concept]], but instead of LED beacons, players wear **retroreflective tape** — the same material used on road signs, safety vests, and running shoes.

Retroreflective tape has a unique property: it bounces light **directly back toward the light source**. When the phone's flashlight (positioned right next to the camera) shines on retroreflective tape, it **blazes brilliantly** in the camera feed, even in a pitch-dark room. But to the naked eye from any other angle, it's nearly invisible.

## How It Works
1. **The Vest:** Each player wears strips of retroreflective tape in a unique pattern/shape on front, back, and shoulders. For example:
   - Player 1: Single horizontal bar
   - Player 2: X shape
   - Player 3: Triangle
   - Player 4: Two vertical bars
   - etc.
2. **The Phone (Gun):** Held in hand with the flashlight ON (set to lowest brightness). The camera feed sees a dark room where ONLY retroreflective patches light up like beacons.
3. **Firing:** Player aims, pulls trigger (volume button). The app captures the frame, finds the bright retroreflective patch in the crosshair, and classifies its shape to identify the player.
4. **Hit Registration:** Same as Idea 9 — server handles all game logic.

## Why This Is Interesting
- **₹0 electronics on the vest** — just tape and scissors.
- **Stealth mechanic:** Retroreflective tape only lights up when someone is LOOKING AT YOU (flashlight near camera). If nobody is pointing their phone at you, you're invisible. This creates a "predator vs prey" dynamic.
- **No batteries on the vest** — nothing to die, nothing to replace.
- **Waterproof, durable** — retroreflective tape is designed for outdoor use.
