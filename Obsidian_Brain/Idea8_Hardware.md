# Idea 8: LED Color Beacon — Hardware

## The LED Beacon Module
**Materials per player:**
- 3x Colored LEDs (red, green, blue — 5mm diffused): ~5 INR
- 1x CR2032 coin cell battery + holder (or 2x AA): ~15 INR
- Small piece of stripboard / direct solder: ~5 INR
- Resistors (330Ω): ~2 INR
- Safety pin or Velcro patch to attach to clothing: ~5 INR

**Assembly:**
1. Solder the LEDs in the player's assigned color combination (e.g., Red-Green for Player 2).
2. Wire to coin cell battery through a simple switch.
3. Mount on a small cardboard or plastic backing with a safety pin.
4. Player pins one on front, one on back. Optionally one on each shoulder for side shots.

**LED Run Time:** A CR2032 can power 2-3 LEDs for 4-6 hours continuously. More than enough for multiple games.
